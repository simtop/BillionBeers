A clean, fast catalog for exploring craft beer.

BillionBeers lets you page through hundreds of beers, dig into the details that actually matter to a brewer or a curious drinker, and keep track of what you still have in stock.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Browse the catalog — scroll a photo-led list of beers with ABV and IBU at a glance. New beers load as you reach the bottom, so there is no paging to fiddle with.

• Search as you type — start typing and the list narrows immediately. No search button, no waiting.

• Browse by style or brewery — jump between beer styles and the breweries that make them, and see how many beers sit under each.

• Read the full spec — every beer has its description, food pairings, ABV, IBU and SRM, release year, serving temperature, fermentation method, ingredients, and the glassware it is best served in.

• Track what is on tap — mark a beer as empty when you run out, and refill the barrels when you restock. Your stock is yours: it is stored on your device and never overwritten by the catalog.

BUILT TO FEEL FAST

Beers you have already seen are cached on your device, so the app opens straight into content instead of a spinner, and keeps working when your connection does not. When something does go wrong, the app tells you what happened and gives you a way to retry, rather than showing an empty screen.

Full support for light and dark themes, and available in English, French and Spanish.

ABOUT THIS APP

BillionBeers is a free, ad-free, open-source app. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Beer data comes from an open beer catalog API, so treat it as a reference for exploring styles and breweries rather than a live shop inventory.

The full source code is on GitHub at github.com/simtop/BillionBeers, where it doubles as a reference project for modern Android development.
